# 👦 About me
<span class='anchor' id='about-me'></span>
<p style="text-align: justify;"> Currently, I am an <strong>Associate Professor</strong> at <a href="https://cs.xidian.edu.cn/">the School of Computer Science and Technology</a>, <a href="https://www.xidian.edu.cn/">Xidian University (XDU, 西安电子科技大学)</a>. I was selected for the <a href="https://www.hkscholars.org/"><strong>2026 Hong Kong Scholars Program (香江学者计划)</strong></a>, where I will work with <a href="https://www.comp.hkbu.edu.hk/~xujl/"><strong>Prof. Jianliang Xu</strong></a>, IEEE Fellow and Chair Professor of Computer Science at Hong Kong Baptist University (HKBU). For more information, please visit my <a href="https://faculty.xidian.edu.cn/caoyukun/zh_CN/index.htm">Official Homepage (官方主页)</a>. I have served as an <strong>Area Chair</strong> for ICLR, ACL, and ICML. I have also served as a reviewer or external reviewer for TPAMI, NeurIPS, ICDE, ICLR, PVLDB, SIGIR, AAAI, IJCAI, and FCS, among other conferences and journals. </p> <p style="text-align: justify;"> I obtained my <strong>Ph.D. degree in Engineering</strong> in November 2025 from <a href="https://cs.ustc.edu.cn/main.htm">the School of Computer Science and Technology</a>, <a href="https://www.ustc.edu.cn/"><strong>the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC, 中国科学技术大学)</strong></a>. During my Ph.D. studies, I worked in the Data Darkness Lab (<a href="http://staff.ustc.edu.cn/~xkxie/index.html">DDL Lab</a>), Center for Medical Imaging, Robotics, Analytic Computing &amp; Learning (<a href="https://miracle.ustc.edu.cn/main.htm">MIRACLE Center</a>), <a href="https://sz.ustc.edu.cn/index.html">Suzhou Institute for Advanced Research</a>, <a href="https://www.ustc.edu.cn/">USTC</a>, under the supervision of <a href="http://staff.ustc.edu.cn/~xkxie/index.html"><strong>Prof. Xike Xie</strong></a>. I joined USTC in September 2020 through postgraduate recommendation (推免入学). Before that, I received my B.E. degree from <a href="http://cis.swu.edu.cn/">the College of Computer and Information Science and College of Software</a>, <a href="http://www.swu.edu.cn/">Southwest University (SWU, 西南大学)</a> in 2020. </p>
